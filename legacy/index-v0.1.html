How to play

1. Tap the map to guide your commander.
2. Use Follow to keep your helpers nearby, Hold to keep their positions, or Move squad to pick a shared destination.
3. Occupy checkpoints. Each zone you control earns points over time.
4. Highest score when the timer ends wins.
